`refactor-design` now applies a pre-implementation decision gate while grounding/grilling or planning a fix: a design decision that's hard to reverse, surprising without context, or a real trade-off — while the candidate itself stays behavior-preserving — gets written as a plan with a proposed default plus an explicit open question on the candidate issue, and the existing `ready-for-agent` label is deliberately withheld until a human confirms or overrides it; `refactor-scan` and the orchestrator skip a still-waiting flagged candidate rather than blocking the whole pass on it. A genuine breaking change discovered mid-design is now handled too, instead of silently falling through: `refactor-design` hands it forward as a finding, and `refactor-learn`'s closing call applies its existing rejection machinery (`wontfix`, a closing note or `out-of-scope/` entry). `CONTEXT.md` gains a `Flagged candidate` entry and widens `Findings` to name `refactor-design` as a second origin.

A JavaScript Pack joins the PHP and Twig Packs: where a project has JavaScript of its own, the suite proposes ESLint with its recommended rules, records today's findings with ESLint's bulk suppressions, clears them part by part, and then takes in `no-var`, `prefer-const` and `object-shorthand` rule by rule. All of it is Guardrails work, so a frontend without a linter never holds the Safety Net back. TypeScript is not covered yet.

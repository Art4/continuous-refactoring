`rector-type-coverage` and `semgrep` now carry a `composer` required parent, alongside their existing recommended parent(s). They were the only two nodes in the whole tooling tree with no required/required-any chain back to `composer` at all, so a rejected `composer` could never automatically close them — on a target with no PHP application code, this forced a manual issue-file-and-reject cycle before `structural-scan` could ever open. A new invariant test now guards against a future edge change silently reintroducing this gap on some other node.

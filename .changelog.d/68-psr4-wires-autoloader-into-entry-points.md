`psr-4` now also wires Composer's autoloader (`vendor/autoload.php`) into the target's own composition root (or every entry point when there's none), on top of its existing check that the mapping mechanism actually works — closing the gap where a target could adopt PSR-4 yet keep loading classes by hand indefinitely, letting a new class silently go missing from a manual require list. `CONTEXT.md` gains `Entry point` and `Composition root` glossary entries.

**Breaking, for 0.7.0:** the suite is rebuilt around one **run** of decision points, with all state in your tracker and forge. `/continuous-refactoring` now leads from your open refactoring tickets to one opened merge request and, at every step — reconcile, Track choice, scan, filing tickets, selecting, design, implementation, merge request — shows what it found, the options and one recommendation. By default you decide each time; say in the call that it should do the run itself ("do it yourself"), or mid-run "carry on yourself from here", and it takes its own recommendations. The call also takes a Track, a ticket or a restriction ("only the Rector tools") in your own words. The suite keeps no state of its own any more: the config file, the bookkeeping document (local and remote), `Ticket-create-mode`, `MR-create-mode` and the Track cadences are gone, and so are the limits of two open merge requests and five open candidates. Tickets are found by their subject instead of a fixed label or title, a ticket you wrote yourself is picked up through a **Candidate** mark or by naming it, and a Track is scanned once — afterwards its tickets are the record. Where your project has its own skills for planning and implementing, a run recommends those and uses the suite's procedures as a fallback. Housekeeping is now one standing ticket made from a template in your repository, with a rhythm you choose, refactoring ideas left on it as comments, and its own command `/continuous-housekeeping` that can be scheduled. The suite is two skills, `continuous-refactoring` and `continuous-housekeeping`; the nine others are removed. The operations in `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` change their cut: **Search** is new and required; **Rejected**, **Blocked by** and **Housekeeping** are new and optional; **Bookkeeping**, **Filed date** and **Comment author and time** are removed.

**A project onboarded on 0.6.0 needs three steps.** (1) Run the onboarding again: call `/continuous-refactoring`, which adds **Search** to your `## Refactoring operations` section and removes the bullets that no longer exist. (2) Delete the suite's old files under `.scratch/refactor/` — `config.md`, `bookkeeping.md`, `merge-requests.md` and the folder `out-of-scope/`; they are no longer read. Tickets kept as local Markdown files under `.scratch/refactor/issues/` stay. To keep your old rejections where they are instead of deleting them, name that folder under **Rejected** in the section (a rejection that should come back by itself once a PHP version is reached needs a line `Blocker: PHP >= X.Y`). Where the bookkeeping lived in a tracker issue, close that issue. (3) Remove the symlinks of the removed skills from your skills folder: `refactor-loop`, `refactor-scan`, `refactor-prioritize`, `refactor-design`, `refactor-implement`, `refactor-learn`, `continuous-safety-net`, `continuous-guardrails`, `continuous-investigation`. Nothing is migrated; the first scan of each Track rebuilds the worklist as tickets.

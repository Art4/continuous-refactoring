The suite now asks whether your project promises stability to code outside it — your package's users, your application's addons — and records where that promise is written (or that there is none) in `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`. Onboarding asks it; a project onboarded before is asked once at the start of its next run. Code the promise covers is restructured with old and new side by side and the old path deprecated the way your promise says; its removal is proposed as an ordinary ticket, never made by a refactoring.

Fixed a bug where a target with a rejected `composer` node (out of scope) could get permanently stuck with zero proposable tooling-tree candidates, never reaching `structural-scan`, even though the rejection should have cascaded down and resolved everything beneath it. The internal gate-closure checks (`_is_effectively_rejected`, `_is_permanently_gated`) mistook a legitimate diamond-shaped dependency (two paths re-converging on a shared ancestor) for a cycle and returned a false negative.

A scan now files tickets only two steps ahead along a chain of tools that block one another: with PHPStan Level 6 in review it files Level 7, not Levels 8 to 10. The scan report names what lies further ahead in one line, and a later scan files it once the steps before it are done, or at once if you name it in the call. A tool's rollout ticket is titled "<Tool>: take in the remaining rules" also when it is filed before the tool is set up.

An autonomous run that stops at the Safety Net now says "Safety Net is unfinished" instead of "Safety Net is waiting for merges", and lists only the Safety Net's own tickets: in review with their merge requests, blocked or waiting with their reasons. Before, merge requests of other Tracks appeared in that list. It also stops only when those tickets account for everything left: a Safety Net tool still to set up without an open ticket makes the run scan for it instead of ending.
